### Partition segments follow their field

The partition path in the Studio editor now references fields by their stable id instead of their name. Renaming a field keeps its segments attached, and two fields sharing a name no longer make a segment ambiguous. The exported `endpoint.json` is unchanged, and segments in an imported file whose name is missing or duplicated are kept and written back as they were. Saved drafts from earlier versions are converted when they load.

### Confirm before removing a partitioned field

Removing a field that the partition path uses, or changing its type so a segment becomes invalid (to Object or Array, or a date component on a non-date type), now asks for confirmation and names the affected segments. Confirming applies the change and removes only those segments in one undo step. Cancelling changes nothing.
