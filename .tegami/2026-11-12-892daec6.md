### Remove keyboard reordering from the Endpoint Builder

Reordering fields is now pointer-only. The "Move field" control and its pick-up, move, drop and cancel keys are gone, along with the keyboard-move hint in the Fields view and keyboard reordering of the partition badges. Adding a field from the palette by keyboard, undo and redo shortcuts, and the command palette are unchanged. After adding a field by keyboard, or jumping to a field from the Validations view, focus now lands in the field's name input.
