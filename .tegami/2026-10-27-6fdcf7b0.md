### create-endpoint can target a specific data source

Endpoint definitions now accept an optional `dataSource` field. `create-endpoint --from-file` passes it through so the generated `config.ts` references the named DataSource instead of always `"default"`. Omitting the field preserves the previous `"default"` behavior.
