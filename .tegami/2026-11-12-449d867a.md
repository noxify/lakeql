### Exact search in the partition picker

The partition picker search matches each typed word as part of a field name, component or type, instead of matching scattered letters. Searching "datetime" no longer lists `Date` fields, and results keep the field order.

### Partition picker opens above its button

The partition picker always opens above the "Add partition field" button and no longer jumps to the other side when you open a date field's components.
