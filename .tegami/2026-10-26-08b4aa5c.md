### Unify LoadStrategy on the datasource definition

`LoadStrategy` now has a single canonical declaration in `@lakeql/datasource`. `@lakeql/storage` and `@lakeql/schema-generator` re-export that type instead of declaring their own identical copies, and each now depends on `@lakeql/datasource`. The exported `LoadStrategy` name and its union (`full_load | full_load_append | append`) are unchanged. `@lakeql/schema-generator` keeps its Zod `loadStrategies` enum and adds a compile-time assertion that the enum and the canonical union cannot drift apart.
