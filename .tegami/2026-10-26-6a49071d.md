### Remove configurable RetryConfig.retryableStatusCodes

`RetryConfig` no longer exposes a `retryableStatusCodes` field. The retryable HTTP status codes (`429, 500, 502, 503, 504`) are now inlined in the retry logic, matching `@lakeql/fabric-client`. Retry behavior is unchanged for the default status codes.

**Note:** the public `RetryConfig` type no longer includes `retryableStatusCodes`; remove it from any `RetryConfig` literal.
