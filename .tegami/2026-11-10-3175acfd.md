### Drag-and-drop canvas with full keyboard authoring

`@lakeql/studio` replaces the Endpoint Builder's tree editor with a drag-and-drop app-builder canvas: a field-type palette, a center canvas of nested field cards, and the existing properties panel. Drag a type from the palette to add a field, drag cards to reorder within a level or move them into and out of `Object` / `Array`-of-`Object` containers, and undo or redo any change from on-screen controls or the standard keyboard shortcuts. Structurally impossible drops (into a non-container, an array of arrays, or a field's own descendant) are rejected with a reason and no state change.

Every structural action is also fully keyboard-operable, with no drag-only exception: focus a field's "Move field" control and press Enter to pick it up, step the target with the arrow keys (each candidate gated live and announced), then Enter to drop or Escape to cancel; focus a palette type and press Enter to insert it into the selected container. Focus is restored to the moved or added field after a commit, and pick-up, target changes, drops, and cancels are announced through a live region. Metadata, mutation config, validations, the type-change dialog, the live JSON view, import (no partial apply), and validity-gated export are unchanged.
