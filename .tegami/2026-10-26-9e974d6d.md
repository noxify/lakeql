### Remove unreachable Error.cause platform guard from DataSourceError

The `DataSourceError` constructor no longer performs an `Error.cause` round-trip check. On supported runtimes the guard could never fire, and if it had, it would have thrown a context-free error that discarded the original failure being reported. The cause is still preserved on the constructed error.
