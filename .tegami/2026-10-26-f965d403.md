### Write pipeline fails closed on an unhandled partitioning mode

The write pipeline's partitioning-mode switch now throws for any mode it does not explicitly handle instead of returning after uploading nothing and running no DDL while reporting success. If a new partitioning mode is ever added without extending the pipeline, the write now fails loudly rather than silently succeeding. `generatePartitionPath` also folds its duplicated fallback into the `"year/month/day"` behavior with no change in output.
