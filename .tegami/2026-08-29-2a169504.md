### Derive `.env.example` sections from adapters and selection order

The generated `.env.example` now labels each section from the contributing feature (the adapter's own name, or "Authentication") instead of guessing from variable prefixes, and lists data-source sections in the order the adapters were selected. New adapters get a correctly labelled section automatically, with no separate label table to maintain.
