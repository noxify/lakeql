### Harden generated Entra ID auth and label auth sections by strategy

The generated Microsoft Entra ID resolver now verifies the token `audience` (against a new `AUTH_CLIENT_ID`) and `issuer`, not just the signature, so it rejects tokens issued for other applications. The `.env.example` authentication section is now labelled by the chosen strategy ("Authentication - Entra ID" or "Authentication - Mock").
