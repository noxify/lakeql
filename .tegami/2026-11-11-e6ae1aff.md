### Metadata validation issues now jump to the matching settings field

In the Validations view, metadata validation issues (table name, catalog, schema, data source, and base path) are now clickable, mirroring field issues. Activating one switches to the Settings view and moves keyboard focus to the corresponding form input, so you can go straight from the issue to the control that fixes it. The read-only `version` entry stays shown but not clickable, since it has no editable input.
