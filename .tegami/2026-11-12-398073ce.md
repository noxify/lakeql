### Date fields only partition by year, month and day

A `Date` value has no time of day, so a partition segment such as `day_of:hour` on a `Date` field is now rejected with a message pointing to `year`, `month` or `day`. `hour`, `minute` and `second` remain available on `DateTime` fields. Studio offers only the valid components for a `Date` field and asks for confirmation before a `DateTime` with a time segment is changed to `Date`.
