### Validate `--auth` against the available strategies

The `--auth` option of `create-lakeql-app` now declares its allowed values (`mock`, `entra-id`) as choices. Invalid values are rejected at parse time with a clear message, and the accepted strategies are exposed in the command metadata so documentation can render them as a structured list instead of free text. The strategy IDs are shared from a single source so the CLI, prompts, and metadata stay in sync.
