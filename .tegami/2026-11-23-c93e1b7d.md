### Searchable partition field picker

The partition field picker is now a popover with a searchable list. Date and DateTime fields open a sub-page with a combined "year / month / day" option and the six single components. Typing "event year" finds `event → year` from the root page. Escape or Backspace on an empty search goes back. Choosing a non-date field adds a bare segment that partitions by raw value. The "Partition granularity" select is gone, along with the `partitioningFormat` setting.
