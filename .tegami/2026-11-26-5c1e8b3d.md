### Adapter choice in the editor

The metadata form has an Adapter select. Trino is selectable. Microsoft Fabric, ClickHouse and DuckDB are listed but disabled until the editor supports them. The choice only shapes the editor, for example the write method control is offered for adapters with more than one write method. It is not part of the exported endpoint definition.
