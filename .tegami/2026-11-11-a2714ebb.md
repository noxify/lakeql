### Restore the active view from the URL hash

The Endpoint Builder now reflects the active view in the URL hash (`#settings`, `#fields`, `#validations`, `#json`). Reloading the page returns to the view you were on, and the views are deep-linkable — a shared link that ends in `#fields` opens straight on the Fields view. Browser back/forward and manual edits to the hash switch the view too. An empty or unrecognized hash falls back to General Settings. The editor's saved definition is unaffected; only the active-view selection lives in the hash.
