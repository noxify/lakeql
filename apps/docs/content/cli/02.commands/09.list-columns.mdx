description: List the columns of a table.
cliCommand: list-columns
---

`list-columns` prints a table's columns and their types, through a configured data source. Use it to inspect a table's shape before you generate an endpoint with [`pull`](/docs/cli/commands/pull).

<Command variant="exec">
  lakeql-cli list-columns --source warehouse --schema sales --table orders
</Command>
